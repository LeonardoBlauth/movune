# Movune brand foundations

## Meaning and position

Movune combines movement and connection. The brand positions the product as a modern operational platform for physiotherapy and Pilates clinics.

**Tagline:** Your clinic in motion.

## Symbol direction

The approved concept is a circle joined to a continuous line that forms an M. It represents connection, continuity and movement without relying on literal healthcare imagery. Crosses, hearts, spines, human silhouettes and similar clinical clichés are intentionally avoided.

No logo asset is published yet. The historical source confirms the concept, but the recoverable public-source package does not include an approved vector file.

## Core palette

- Brand 500: `#0F9F8F`
- Brand 600: `#0B8176`
- Brand 700: `#09685F`
- Brand 50: `#ECFDF9`
- Ink: `#17211F`
- Muted text: `#66736F`
- Border: `#E5E8E8`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Canvas: `#F8FAF9`

Brand colors identify Movune. Semantic colors for status, warnings, errors, physiotherapy and Pilates remain independent.

## Typography

- Manrope for brand and headings;
- Inter for interface and long-form content;
- system fallbacks when web fonts are unavailable.

## Usage principles

- keep product identity distinct from the clinic's own branding;
- preserve contrast and readable hierarchy in light and dark themes;
- communicate status and service through text and shape as well as color;
- use green as an accent in dark mode rather than tinting every surface.
